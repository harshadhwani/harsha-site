Bhajana Bharatha

ಭಜನ ಭಾರತ

One evening, the whole of Bharata in song

A conceptualised concert of bhajans, devotional songs, Sanskrit chants and theatre music, sung by ShreeHarsha with his team against living backdrops of temples, deities and sacred cities.

I

Roaring Rama

The voice of Sanatana Dharma as a rap in Sanskrit. ShreeHarsha wrote and composed it in February 2022; at Bhajana Bharata it became the night's anthem.
